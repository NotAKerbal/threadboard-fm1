# Threadboard branding

<img src="assets/threadboard-icon.png" width="128" height="128" alt="Threadboard's three rounded keys forming a T">

**Threadboard**

Your threads, at hand.

The mark forms a T from three rounded keys. Its charcoal keycaps and warm
cream surroundings come from the physical FM-1. Amber highlights the control
or action being explained.

## Palette and type

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Warm cream background | `#F3EEDC` |
| Charcoal text and keycaps | `#222825` |
| Amber annotations | `#CB6928` |

Use a system sans-serif for titles and body copy, and a system monospace for
control labels. Keep the Threadboard name in sentence case. Use generous space
around the icon, and place the charcoal mark on a light background.

Amber is a documentation accent. Hardware status is communicated through light
brightness and flashing, as described in the [interaction guide](interactions.md).
